Straight Answers

Questions we get daily

How does the broker fee work?

Flat $1,500 on sourced vehicles, only when we win your car within your max bid. No win, no fee. The estimator above already includes it — the number you see is the number you pay, plus tax, title, and transport at cost.

Can I really see the auction data?

Yes. Before we bid, we sit down with recent sale prices for the exact year, trim, and mileage band — the same data dealers use. You set the max bid; we never exceed it.

What's included in fleet pricing?

Insurance-ready inspected vehicles, maintenance, roadside swap, and full tire care (tires, rotations, flat repair) through TreadZone Tire & Wheel. You fuel it and drive it.

What if the car has problems when it arrives?

Every unit runs the full TreadZone Certified line before handoff — fluids replaced, fresh oil, new tires, brakes and rotors inspected, timing components verified, and paint corrected where needed. If something material was still missed in the auction condition report, we make it right or run the arbitration process with the auction house on your behalf.

Do you rent to businesses only?

No — the daily rental fleet is open to anyone 21+ with a license and card. The fleet program (multi-vehicle, multi-month) is where the business pricing kicks in.
